# VirtualWorks-Lab Pharmacovigilance Internship

## Task 2 – ADR Reporting & Documentation

**Internship Domain:** Pharmacovigilance  
**Task ID:** task2  
**Topic:** ADR Reporting & Documentation

## Objective

The objective of this task is to complete a structured Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) report based on a simulated patient case.

The case was assessed by reviewing:

- Patient information
- Suspected medication
- Adverse reaction
- Date/timeline of the reaction
- Outcome
- Seriousness
- Action taken
- Reporter information

## Patient Case

- Patient: Female
- Age: 45 years
- Reported symptoms:
  - Rash
  - Itching
  - Nausea
  - Dizziness

## ADR Assessment

The reported symptoms were reviewed in relation to medication exposure and the timing of the reaction.

The suspected reaction includes dermatological and gastrointestinal/general symptoms. A temporal relationship between the suspected medication and the onset of symptoms should be considered during causality assessment.

## Suspected ADR

Possible adverse reactions:

1. Skin rash
2. Itching
3. Nausea
4. Dizziness

## Reporting Process

The ADR reporting process followed these steps:

1. Identify the patient.
2. Record the suspected medicine.
3. Document the adverse reaction.
4. Record the onset and recovery timeline.
5. Assess seriousness.
6. Record the outcome.
7. Document action taken regarding the suspected medicine.
8. Add reporter information.
9. Review the report for completeness.

## Conclusion

This task provided practical understanding of how an ADR case is documented using a structured pharmacovigilance reporting format.

It helped demonstrate the importance of accurate patient information, medication history, adverse-event description, timeline assessment and proper documentation.

## Key Learning

Through this task, I learned the basic workflow of ADR reporting and documentation in pharmacovigilance.
